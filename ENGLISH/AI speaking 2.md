# New Vocabulary

## prospect
**Part of speech:** noun

### Meaning
The possibility that something good will happen in the future.

### Examples

- I don't see many prospects in changing frameworks.
- This company has good career prospects.

### Common collocations

- career prospects
- future prospects
- job prospects
- good prospects
- poor prospects

### Notes

Today we corrected:

❌ more perspectives

✅ better prospects

---

## benefit
**Part of speech:** noun

### Meaning

An advantage or something useful that you gain.

### Examples

- I don't see much benefit in changing frameworks.
- There is a real benefit to learning TypeScript.

### Common collocations

- see the benefit
- get a benefit
- benefit from
- long-term benefit

---

## opportunity
**Part of speech:** noun

### Meaning

A chance to do something.

### Examples

- It could be a good opportunity.
- I'm waiting for a better opportunity.

---

## transferable skills

### Meaning

Skills that can be used in different jobs or technologies.

### Examples

- Architecture is a transferable skill.
- TypeScript knowledge is highly transferable.

---

## architecture
**Part of speech:** noun

### Meaning

The way software is designed and organized.

### Examples

- I enjoy software architecture.
- Good architecture makes projects easier to maintain.

---

## gradually

# New Collocations

## make progress

### Meaning

To improve.

### Examples

- I want to make progress every day.
- I'm making progress with my English.

---

## stick to a plan

### Meaning

To continue following your plan without giving up.

### Examples

- I try to stick to my plan.
- It's difficult to stick to a plan when you're tired.

---

## step by step

### Meaning

Gradually, one stage after another.

### Examples

- I'm learning English step by step.
- We'll solve this step by step.

---

## take a break

### Meaning

To stop working for a short time.

### Examples

- Let's take a break.
- I usually take a short break after work.

---

## keep in mind

### Meaning

To remember something important.

### Examples

- Keep in mind that consistency is important.
- Keep this in mind during the interview.

---

## pay attention to

### Meaning

To carefully notice something.

### Examples

- Pay attention to pronunciation.
- Pay attention to the details.

---

# Conjunctions

## even though

### Meaning

Despite the fact that.

### Structure

Even though + clause, clause.

### Examples

- Even though I was tired, I studied English.
- Even though I had a headache, I made coffee later.

---

## unless

### Meaning

If not.

### Examples

- I'll practice English unless something stops me.
- You won't improve unless you practice.

---

## whereas

### Meaning

Used to compare two different ideas.

### Examples

- I enjoy programming, whereas my wife enjoys teaching.
- React is popular, whereas Vue is simpler for many developers.

---

# Grammar Corrections

## ❌ make a progress

✅ make progress

"Progress" is uncountable.

---

## ❌ My education should be step by step.

More natural:

✅ My learning should be step by step.

or

✅ I want to learn step by step.

---

## ❌ Pay attention on the rules.

✅ Pay attention to the rules.

---

## ❌ I agree.

(when agreeing with someone)

✅ I agree with him.

---

## ❌ Is it any benefit to learn React?

✅ Is there any benefit to learning React?

or

✅ Do you think it's worth learning React?

---

## ❌ My salary will be smaller.

✅ My salary would be lower.

---

## ❌ I don't see many perspectives.

✅ I don't see many prospects.

or

✅ I don't see much benefit.
