# New Vocabulary

## drained
**Meaning**

Extremely tired mentally or physically.

### Examples

- After six hours of debugging I felt completely drained.
- I'm mentally drained.

### Better than

❌ very tired

---

## topic

Today we corrected:

❌ theme

✅ topic

Examples:

- This topic has been important to me for a long time.
- Today's topic was interesting.

---

## burner

The cooking area on a stove.

Examples:

- All the burners are busy.
- Please leave one burner free.

---

## free up

### Phrasal verb

Meaning:

To make something available.

Examples:

- Could you free up one burner?
- We need to free up some memory.

---

## absorb

Meaning:

To learn something deeply.

Examples:

- It takes time to absorb new grammar.
- I need time to absorb everything.

---

## internalize

Meaning:

To make knowledge become natural.

Examples:

- I want to internalize these collocations.
- Eventually you'll internalize these structures.

---

## let it sink in

### Idiom

Meaning:

Give yourself time to fully understand something.

Examples:

- I'll read the summary and let it sink in.

---

## fascinating

Meaning:

Extremely interesting.

Examples:

- That's fascinating.
- I find languages fascinating.

---

## intriguing

Meaning:

Interesting because it makes you curious.

Examples:

- That's intriguing.
- An intriguing idea.

---

## pretty cool

Very natural conversational English.

Examples:

- That's pretty cool.
- Your idea is pretty cool.

# New Collocations

## important to me

NOT

❌ important for me

Examples:

- This topic has been important to me for years.
- Music is important to me.

---

## make coffee

NOT

❌ cook coffee

Examples:

- I make coffee every morning.

---

## cook dinner

Examples:

- I usually cook dinner.

---

## make dinner

Also correct.

Difference:

Cook = focus on preparing food with heat.

Make = general preparation.

---

## make a sandwich

---

## make ice cream

---

## process information

Examples:

- I need time to process new ideas.

---

## get lost

Meaning:

Lose track of the explanation.

Examples:

- Sorry, Jenny, I got lost.
- I couldn't follow that.

Excellent phrase for our lessons.

---

## stand out

Meaning:

Be different in a positive way.

Examples:

- Your CV should stand out.
- This candidate stands out.

# Grammar

## No one / Nobody / Nothing / None

### no one

Only people.

Examples:

- No one called me.

---

### nobody

Same meaning.

Examples:

- Nobody knows.

---

### nothing

Things.

Examples:

- There was nothing in the box.

---

### none

Groups.

Examples:

- None of the burners is free.

--- 

### not a single

Examples:

- Not a single word should be in Russian in this chat.

# Phrasal Verbs

## free up

make available

---

## eat up

finish completely

---

## use up

consume completely

Examples:

- We've used up all the coffee.

Important note:

"Up" doesn't have one fixed meaning.

Each phrasal verb has its own meaning.
