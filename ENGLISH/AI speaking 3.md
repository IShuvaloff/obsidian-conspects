# Grammar

## Present Perfect vs Past Simple

Today's biggest grammar topic.

---

### Past Simple

Use when the action happened at a finished time.

Examples

- Today I learned BFF.
- I finished my work.
- I made progress.

---

### Present Perfect

Use when the result is important now.

Examples

- I've just finished work.
- I've learned the basics of BFF.
- I've made progress today.

---

### Excellent examples you created

✅ I've just finished work.

✅ I've just cooked dinner.

✅ I've made progress today.

# Replacing "Very"

One thing we noticed today is that I often use **very**.

Native speakers often replace **very + adjective** with either:

- a stronger adjective;
- another intensifier.

---

## Instead of **very happy**

✅ delighted

✅ thrilled

✅ pleased

---

## Instead of **very satisfied**

✅ really satisfied

✅ truly satisfied

✅ delighted

---

## Instead of **very useful**

✅ really useful

✅ extremely useful

✅ incredibly useful

---

## Instead of **very interesting**

✅ fascinating

✅ engaging

✅ captivating

---

## Instead of **very beautiful**

✅ gorgeous - warm admiration (искреннее восхищение)

✅ stunning - dramatic impact (сильное впечатление)

---

## Instead of **very tired**

## Instead of **very good**

# Teacher's Advice

Don't try to remove **very** completely.

Native speakers use it all the time.

The goal is simply to have **more options**.

If you know five different ways to express the same idea, your speech immediately sounds richer and more natural.
